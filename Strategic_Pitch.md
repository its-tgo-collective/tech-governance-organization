# TGO & ITS: An Institutional and Technical Lever for Europe's Tech Sovereignty

**To:** EU Policymakers, Think Tanks (e.g., Bruegel, Rhine Group), and Stakeholders in the Draghi Implementation Circle.
**From:** The TGO & ITS Collective
**Subject:** A new mechanism to enforce tech sovereignty without bureaucratic overreach.

---

## 1. The Problem (Why the EU is currently losing)
The EU is trying to curb the power of US hyperscalers (Cloud Act, CADA, AI Act) with slow legislative processes. At the same time, critical open-source infrastructure is maintained by unpaid maintainers whose work is being absorbed by AI corporations without compensation. 
*   **The result:** Politics is too slow, corporations are too powerful, and regulatory attempts are diluted by massive lobbying (Regulatory Capture).

## 2. The Solution (Two complementary pillars)
We propose a paradigm shift: Instead of relying solely on state enforcement, we introduce an independent, self-funded institutional body (TGO) backed by a hardware-enforced technical mechanism (ITS).

### Pillar 1: The TGO (Tech-Governance-Organization)
An independent, civil-society-funded network. It is not an EU body, but a "lightning rod." The TGO takes over the unpopular tasks of standard-setting, auditing, and sanctioning. It relieves the EU without violating its sovereign rights. States can refer to the TGO to withstand corporate lobbying pressure.

### Pillar 2: The ITS (Individual Tech Seal)
The technical weapon of the TGO (already published as Open Source with a DOI). A hardware-bound licensing framework that protects works (code, art, data) from unauthorized AI training. It enforces the creator's rules autonomously at the chip level (Z6 Vector).

## 3. The Leverage (How this benefits the EU)
*   **Automatism instead of Negotiation:** The TGO creates rules that cannot be diluted by lobbying. The ITS enforces them on a hardware level. If a corporation violates the rules (e.g., unauthorized AI scraping), the Smart Contract automatically fines them.
*   **Positive Incentives instead of just Fines:** The TGO does not just punish (like the GDPR); it rewards compliance. It offers supply chain prioritization, resource sharing, and coordinated funding for European SMEs. This creates fair competition.
*   **Cushioning for International Organizations:** ITU, ISO, and IEEE can refer to the TGO as an independent reference authority to escape national influence.

## 4. The Call (What we need from the EU)
We do not ask for funding. We ask for **recognition**. 
1.  Acknowledge the TGO as an independent reference authority for tech governance.
2.  Align the legal framework (e.g., in the Data Act, CADA) so that the positive incentives of the TGO can take effect. 

The TGO is ready to take the responsibility for standard-setting that politics cannot bear.
